01 — WHO WE ARE

Who We Are

RESAP is an online document utility platform designed to help students, job seekers and professionals prepare digital documents for online applications.

From resizing photographs and signatures to compressing images and managing PDFs, we bring essential document preparation tools together in one convenient place.

02 — OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify everyday document preparation by providing easy-to-use tools that help people prepare their files for online applications.

We want to make useful document utilities accessible without complicated software or unnecessary steps.
