Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

At RESAP (Resize. Prepare. Apply.), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website and use our tools.

1. About RESAP

RESAP is an online document utility platform designed to help students, job seekers and professionals prepare documents for online applications.

Our services include image resizing, image compression, image conversion, signature preparation and PDF-related tools. You can use our website without creating an account.

2. Information We Collect

RESAP does not require users to register or provide personal information to access its basic tools.

Depending on how you interact with our website, the following information may be processed:

  • Uploaded files: Images and PDF documents selected for processing through our tools.
  • Contact information: Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us by email.
  • Technical information: Basic information such as browser type, device type, IP address and website access logs may be collected by our hosting or infrastructure providers.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

3. How We Process Your Files

RESAP aims to process documents efficiently while minimizing unnecessary data collection.

Our Image Resizer and Image Compressor currently process uploaded images directly in your browser using browser-based technologies.

  • Files are loaded locally in your browser.
  • Image processing takes place using browser capabilities such as HTML Canvas.
  • Processed files are generated in your browser for download.

Other RESAP tools may use different processing methods. Please do not assume that every tool follows the same processing method. We will update this policy if our file-processing practices change.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed or received through RESAP may be used to:

  • Provide and operate website features.
  • Respond to questions, feedback and support requests.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.
  • Identify and fix technical issues.
  • Improve the performance and usability of our services.

We do not sell users' personal information.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

RESAP or its hosting and service providers may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security or other disclosed purposes.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

If analytics or advertising services are introduced, this policy should be updated to identify those services and explain their data practices.

6. Third-Party Services

RESAP may rely on third-party providers for services such as website hosting, infrastructure, analytics or other website functionality.

These providers may process certain technical information as necessary to provide their services.

Third-party websites and services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies when using external services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through RESAP.

However, no website, internet connection or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security. Users should avoid uploading highly sensitive documents unless they are comfortable with the processing method used by the particular tool.

8. Children's Privacy

RESAP is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for account registration or other unnecessary purposes.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request information about personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
