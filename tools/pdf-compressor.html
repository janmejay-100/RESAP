How to compress a PDF

Upload your PDF, select a compression level and resolution, or enter a custom target size in KB. Start compression and review the resulting file size before downloading.

Does PDF compression affect quality?

Compression can reduce image quality depending on the selected settings. Image-based compression may also convert document pages into images, which can affect text selection and searching.
