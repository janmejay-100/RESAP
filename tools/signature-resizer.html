What is a Signature Resizer?

RESAP Signature Resizer helps you resize handwritten signature images to the required dimensions for online applications, government forms, examinations and other document submissions. Prepare your signature image quickly without installing software or creating an account.

How to resize a signature online?

Upload your signature image in JPG, PNG or WebP format. Enter the required width and height, choose whether to maintain the original aspect ratio, and select your preferred output format. Preview the resized signature and download the result.

Which image formats are supported?

You can prepare signature images using supported formats such as JPG, PNG and WebP. Choose the output format according to the requirements of the application portal where you plan to submit your signature.

Can I resize a signature for government applications?

Yes. You can prepare signature images for online forms, examination applications and government portals. However, each application may specify different image dimensions, file size limits and formats. Always check the official instructions before submitting your signature.

Is the Signature Resizer free?

RESAP provides free document preparation tools without requiring an account. You can resize and download your signature image directly from your browser.
