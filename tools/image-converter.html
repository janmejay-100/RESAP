How to convert an image

Upload an image, select your desired output format, choose the quality where applicable, and convert it. Preview the result before downloading.

Which image format should you choose?

JPG is commonly used for photographs, PNG supports transparency, and WebP can provide smaller files with good visual quality.

Does converting an image reduce quality?

Quality loss depends on the output format and settings. Converting to JPG or WebP can involve lossy compression. PNG output is lossless, although converting an already compressed image cannot restore lost detail.
