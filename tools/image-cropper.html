How to crop an image

Upload an image, select the area you want to keep, choose an aspect ratio if required, and crop your image. Preview the result before downloading.

What is an aspect ratio?

An aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between an image's width and height. Common ratios include 1:1, 3:4 and 16:9.
