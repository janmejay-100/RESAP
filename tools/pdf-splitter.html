How to split PDF files

Upload your PDF document, choose a splitting method, and click Split PDF. Download the generated files individually.

Can I extract specific PDF pages?

Yes. Enter the page numbers or ranges you want to extract. For example, 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Can I split every page separately?

Yes. Select the option to split every page separately. Each page will be saved as an individual PDF file.
