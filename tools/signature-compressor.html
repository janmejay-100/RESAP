Compression Summary

What is a Signature Compressor?

RESAP Signature Compressor helps you reduce the file size of handwritten signature images for online applications, examination forms, government portals and other document submissions. Compress your signature image while keeping it clear and readable.

How to compress a signature image online?

Upload your signature image to RESAP Signature Compressor. Adjust the compression settings if available, preview the result and download the compressed image. You can reduce the file size to meet the upload limits specified by an application form.

Why do I need to compress my signature?

Many online application forms specify a maximum file size for signature uploads. If your image is too large, it may not upload successfully. Compressing the image can help you meet the required file-size limit.

How can I maintain signature quality while compressing?

Avoid excessive compression because it can make handwritten signatures blurry or difficult to read. Always preview the compressed image before downloading and check that the signature remains clear.

Can I use the compressed signature for government applications?

Yes, you can prepare a signature image for online applications. However, every examination, recruitment process and government portal may have different image dimensions, file formats and file-size requirements. Always check the official instructions before uploading.

Is RESAP Signature Compressor free?

Yes. RESAP provides a free and easy-to-use signature compression tool. You can prepare your signature image online without creating an account.
