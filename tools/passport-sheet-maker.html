What is a Passport Photo Sheet Maker?

RESAP Passport Photo Sheet Maker helps you arrange multiple passport-size photographs on a single printable sheet. Upload your photograph, choose the required photo dimensions, select a paper size and customize the spacing between photos.

How to create a passport photo sheet?

Upload your passport photograph in JPG, PNG or WebP format. Select a photo size such as 35 × 45 mm or 2 × 2 inches. Choose your paper size, orientation, print resolution and number of photographs. Adjust the spacing and margins, generate your print sheet and download it in PDF, JPG or PNG format.

Which paper sizes are supported?

RESAP supports A4, A5 and 4 × 6 inch paper sizes. You can choose portrait or landscape orientation and customize the layout according to your printing requirements.

Can I create multiple passport photos on one page?

Yes. You can specify how many photographs you want on the sheet. RESAP automatically arranges them based on the selected photo dimensions, paper size, margins and spacing.

Is the Passport Photo Sheet Maker free?

RESAP provides free document preparation tools without requiring an account. You can create and download printable passport photo sheets directly from your browser.
