What is a Passport Photo Editor?

RESAP Passport Photo Editor helps you prepare passport-size photographs for online applications, examinations, government forms and other official document requirements. Upload your photograph and adjust its dimensions to create an application-ready image.

How to resize a passport-size photo?

Upload your photograph in JPG, PNG or WebP format. Choose the required photo dimensions, adjust the width and height, and maintain the aspect ratio if needed. Preview your edited photograph and download the final image.

What passport photo sizes are supported?

You can prepare photographs according to the dimensions required by your application. Always check the official application guidelines for the correct photo dimensions, file size and format before uploading your photograph.

Can I use the edited photo for government applications?

Yes. You can use the prepared photograph for applications that accept its dimensions and file format. However, different government examinations and application portals may have different requirements, so verify those requirements before submission.

Is the Passport Photo Editor free?

RESAP provides free document preparation tools without requiring an account. You can prepare and download your passport-size photograph directly from your browser.
