How to compress an image

Upload an image, choose your preferred quality or enter a target file size, and compress it. Review the resulting file size before downloading.

Does image compression reduce quality?

Lossy compression can reduce visual quality. Lower quality settings generally produce smaller files. Results depend on the original image, format and compression settings.
